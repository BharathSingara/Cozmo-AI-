# Unedited six-minute demo recording checklist (recording outstanding)

Use a real room with permission, containing ≥60 actual books on ≥2 shelving units and ≥8 non-book items. Open the deployed browser app on a device with HTTPS or `localhost` camera permission. Set country/currency and provider keys beforehand. Begin an **unedited single-screen recording** before clicking Start, and do not stitch or speed up footage.

0:00–0:20 Show configured country, currency and blank live inventory. 0:20–0:35 Start capture, hear agent greeting. 0:35–2:30 Walk through all spines, both shelves and visible contents; show counts moving live, one blur or glare warning, and a successful voice correction/exclusion. Do not cut. 2:30–3:00 Show every wall/corner and room measurement status; finish. 3:00–5:30 Show final processing, review queue, total (including unpriced), JSON and HTML source links, evidence images. 5:30–6:00 Show validation/latency and conclude. If processing runs longer, document the miss rather than editing around it.

Record actual screen/audio hardware and market provider calls; a synthetic offline/API recording must not be presented as live accuracy proof. Keep personal/private data out of the clip.
